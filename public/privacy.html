Privacy policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

This page explains what Commish collects, why, and what you can do about it. The short version: your TikTok export files never leave your device. We only keep your email address and whether you're on Free or Pro.

Who we are

Commish is run by Jasper Living Limited (company number 16664307, registered office 82a James Carter Road, Mildenhall, United Kingdom, IP28 7DE). We are the data controller for the personal data described here. You can reach us at hello@commish.fyi.

Your export files

When you add a TikTok affiliate orders export, it is read by your own browser on your own device. The file and the order details in it are never uploaded to us and never stored on our servers. We can't see them.

If you add files and then leave to sign in or pay, your browser keeps a temporary copy on your device so you don't have to add them again. It's deleted as soon as you're back, or after three hours.

What we collect

  • Your email address, so you can sign in with an emailed link.
  • Your plan (Free or Pro) and, if you subscribe, your Stripe customer and subscription references, billing period and renewal date.
  • Basic account dates: when you joined and when you last visited.
  • Sign-in links and sessions, stored only as scrambled codes. Unused sign-in links are deleted within a day; sessions end after 30 days or when you sign out.

We don't collect your name, address, phone number or card details.

Payments

Payments are handled by Stripe. Your card details go straight to Stripe and never reach us. Stripe collects your billing details to take payment and prevent fraud, under its own privacy policy.

Emails

We email you sign-in links and essential messages about your account or subscription. We send these through Resend, from servers in Ireland. We won't send you marketing unless you've said yes to it.

Visitor stats

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and see which pages are used. It doesn't use cookies, doesn't track you across other sites and doesn't identify you. We only see totals.

Cookies and storage

  • commish_session: a strictly necessary cookie that keeps you signed in. It's only set when you sign in.
  • Your browser's storage: remembers whether you chose dark mode, and holds the temporary copy of your files described above.

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies, so there's no cookie banner.

Who helps us run Commish

  • Cloudflare: hosts the website, our small account database and the visitor stats.
  • Stripe: takes payments and manages subscriptions.
  • Resend: sends our emails.

Some of these companies may process data outside the UK. Where they do, they use legal safeguards approved under UK law, such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or the UK-US data bridge.

Why we're allowed to use your data

  • To give you your account and your subscription (a contract with you).
  • To keep Commish secure and understand how it's used, in a way that doesn't identify you (our legitimate interests).
  • To keep payment records for tax (a legal obligation).

How long we keep it

We keep your account while you have one. If you ask us to delete it, we'll do so within 30 days. Payment records are kept for six years because HMRC requires it.

Your rights

You can ask to see the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, take a copy, or object to how we use it. Email hello@commish.fyi from the address on your account and we'll reply within a month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk. We'd appreciate the chance to put it right first.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll update the date at the top and, for big changes, email you.
